# HR Analytics Dashboard

## Project Overview

The **HR Analytics Dashboard** is an interactive Tableau project designed to analyze employee attrition, workforce demographics, and job satisfaction. The dashboard helps HR professionals identify factors contributing to employee turnover and supports data-driven workforce planning.

---

## Objectives

- Analyze employee attrition across departments.
- Understand workforce distribution by age and education.
- Evaluate job satisfaction across different job roles.
- Compare attrition trends by gender and age.
- Identify key insights to improve employee retention.

---

## Dashboard KPIs

- **Employee Count:** 1,470
- **Attrition Count:** 237
- **Attrition Rate:** 16.12%
- **Active Employees:** 1,233
- **Average Employee Age:** 37 Years

## Key Insights

- Sales department has the highest employee attrition.
- Employees aged around 30–36 years make up the largest workforce segment.
- Life Sciences has the highest attrition among all education fields.
- Male employees have a higher attrition count than female employees.
- Job satisfaction varies across different job roles.

---

## Business Questions & Answers

### 1. Which department experiences the highest attrition?

**Answer:**
The **Sales** department has the highest employee attrition with **133 employees (56.12%)**, followed by **R&D (92 employees, 38.82%)** and **HR (12 employees, 5.06%)**.

---

### 2. Which age group has the largest employee population?

**Answer:**
Employees aged **33 years** form the largest workforce group with **213 employees**, followed by **30 years (190 employees)** and **36 years (177 employees)**.

---

### 3. How does attrition differ by gender?

**Answer:**
**Male employees** have a higher attrition count (**150**) than **Female employees (87)**, indicating higher employee turnover among males.

---

### 4. Which education field contributes most to employee turnover?

**Answer:**
**Life Sciences** records the highest attrition (**89 employees**), followed by **Medical (63)**, **Marketing (35)**, **Technical Degree (32)**, and **Other (11)**.

---

### 5. Which job roles have lower job satisfaction?

**Answer:**
Job satisfaction varies across roles. Based on the dashboard, **Human Resources**, **Healthcare Representative**, and **Manufacturing Director** show comparatively lower satisfaction ratings than roles such as **Sales Executive** and **Research Scientist**.

---

### 6. What insights can HR use to improve employee retention?

**Answer:**
- Focus retention strategies on the **Sales department**, where attrition is the highest.
- Investigate the reasons behind the higher attrition among **male employees**.
- Improve employee engagement and career development for **Life Sciences** and **Medical** graduates.
- Increase job satisfaction through recognition programs, training, and career growth opportunities.
- Pay special attention to employees in the **30–36 age group**, as they represent the largest portion of the workforce.
